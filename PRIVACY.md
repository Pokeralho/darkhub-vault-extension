# Privacy policy: DarkHub Vault browser extension

_Last updated: 2026-10-28_

**DarkHub Vault collects nothing and sends nothing over the internet.**

- The extension talks only to the DarkHub Suite app on your own computer, through the browser's native messaging. It
  has no server, no account, no analytics, no telemetry and no ads.
- To show and fill logins, it sends the address of the page you are on to DarkHub on your PC, which answers with the
  usernames saved for that site. A password is sent to the page only when you click Fill (or pick a login from the
  icon in a login field), and only to that page's site.
- When you sign in to a site, the extension can offer to save the login. It is sent to DarkHub on your PC only when you
  click "Send to DarkHub", and DarkHub asks you again before saving it. Until you answer, that login waits in the
  browser's session storage, which lives in memory and is cleared when the browser closes. You can turn the offer off
  in the popup.
- The extension stores in the browser only its pairing token (which lets DarkHub recognize it) and your "offer to save
  logins" setting. Removing the extension or unpairing it deletes them.
- Nothing is sold, shared or transferred to anyone.

Source code: <https://github.com/Pokeralho/darkhub-vault-extension>. Questions: open an issue there.
